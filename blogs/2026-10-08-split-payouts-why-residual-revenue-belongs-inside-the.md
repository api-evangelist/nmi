Growing your portfolio should make revenue easier to manage, not harder. For most independent sales organizations (ISOs), the opposite happens. Every merchant you board carries its own pricing, every agent and referral partner carries its own split, and every value-added solution (VAS) you resell adds another party owed a cut.
